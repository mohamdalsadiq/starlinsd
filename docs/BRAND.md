# Slotra

One-word product name for a local subscription slot, time and revenue manager. This is a product identity choice, not a claim of trademark clearance.

Palette: evergreen #062F2A, mint #70EDC3, ivory, warm gold. Launcher mark: interlocking S/hourglass. Native 48dp interactive targets, RTL, legible financial typography, restrained movement.

Generated with the built-in image generator; copied unchanged into Android drawable-nodpi. Android adaptive masks and a vector monochrome fallback handle launcher presentation.

Prompt: Production Android launcher icon for Slotra. Square opaque full-bleed deep evergreen. Bold geometric monogram of two flowing interlocking bands forming an S, subtly suggesting an hourglass and connection, ivory and bright mint with a purposeful warm gold accent. Premium modern utility identity, readable at small sizes. Centered safe zone, no text, phone, mockup, border or busy detail.
